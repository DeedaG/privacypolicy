Bucket Quest Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Information used by the app

Bucket Quest uses account information, saved bucket-list plans, collaborator details, comments, research options, checklist items, itinerary items, and booking records that users choose to enter into the app.

Backend services

Bucket Quest uses Supabase for authentication, database storage, and access control. Plan data is protected with row-level security so users can access plans they own or are invited to collaborate on.

AI plan builder

When a Premium user chooses to use the AI plan builder, the prompt they enter is sent through Bucket Quest's Supabase backend to OpenAI to generate a draft. Do not include sensitive personal information in an AI prompt. AI drafts may be inaccurate and should be reviewed before they are saved or used.

Photos and documents

Photos, booking documents, captions, and related information that users choose to upload are stored with Supabase so they can be accessed by authorized trip participants. Users should only upload content they have permission to share.

Safety screening of shared content

With your consent, Bucket Quest sends submitted shared text and supported photos through its Supabase backend to OpenAI's moderation service to check for potentially harmful content. This includes new submissions and edits, trip descriptions, comments, captions, names displayed to other travelers, and related shared planning text. Account email fields, subscription receipts, and payment amount fields are excluded from screening requests. Avoid entering sensitive personal information into shared text or photos.

Submissions remain private to their submitter until approved. Flagged content, unsupported files, and items that cannot be screened remain private for review. Automated decisions can be mistaken. Contact christigamesllc@gmail.com to request review or appeal. Screening status and decision information are stored privately in Supabase. Declining screening keeps the current draft without saving the requested shared submission. To withdraw consent for future screening, contact support; this does not recall information already processed.

Bookings

Bucket Quest does not book travel, tickets, lodging, or activities directly in the current version. The app may link to third-party providers, and any booking made with those providers is subject to their policies.

Purchases

In-app subscriptions are processed by Apple. Christi Games, LLC does not receive full payment-card information.

Sharing

Christi Games, LLC does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate the app, process purchases, maintain security, or comply with law.

Retention and deletion

Account and planning information is retained while an account remains active so Bucket Quest can provide its saving and collaboration features. A signed-in user can select Delete Account in the Account section of the app. Account deletion permanently removes the account and associated profile, owned plans, photos, documents, comments, and other saved content, except information that must be retained to meet legal, security, or fraud-prevention obligations. Deleting a Bucket Quest account does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription, which must be managed separately through the user's App Store account settings.

Contact

Questions may be sent to christigamesllc@gmail.com.

Safety reports and blocked people

If you report shared content, Bucket Quest stores your concern and a copy of the reported text or the private photo's storage reference for support review. Reports are not shown to other travelers. Blocking stores the blocked account and separates shared trip access. Unblocking does not automatically rejoin trips. Contact christigamesllc@gmail.com about reports or safety concerns.
